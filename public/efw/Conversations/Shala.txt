#Q: I wasn't here back then. What did they do?

##Q: What changes did they make?
  A: They repainted all the accomodation units, laid down colorful fake grass on the bare earth, and planted hundreds of trees. All so they could say, "Look! This place isn't nearly so bad as everyone thinks!"
##Q: What happened to the children?
  A: For the first time since they were locked up, they were taken outside the camp for an excursion. This was the only activity available to them for months! And we were all called by our real names for the first time. It's dehumanising enough for us, but imagine how it must feel for the children, addressed as just a number every day.
#Q: The UN can't help us. From what I've heard, they compile reports and say that incarcerating us like this is wrong, but what changes? Nothing.
